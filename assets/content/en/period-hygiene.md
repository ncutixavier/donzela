Keeping clean during your period protects your health and your confidence. Whatever you use, the rules are the same: keep it clean, change it often and keep the area dry.

## Pads
- Change a pad every **4 to 8 hours**, or sooner if it is full.
- Wash your hands before and after changing.
- Wipe from front to back.
- Throw used pads in a bin, not in the toilet.

## Reusable cloths or pads
- Rinse in cold water first, then wash with soap.
- Dry fully in sunlight before using again. Damp cloth can cause smell and infection.
- Keep a clean spare set, and store them dry.

## Tampons and cups
- Change a tampon every **4 to 8 hours**. Never leave one in for more than 8 hours.
- A cup can be emptied every 8 to 12 hours. Wash it with clean water before reinserting.
- If you feel very unwell with fever, vomiting or dizziness while using a tampon, remove it and seek care at once.

## Washing
- Wash the outside of your genitals with clean water once a day. Do not douche or use strong soap inside the vagina.
- Wear clean, breathable underwear, and change it daily or when wet.

## Carry supplies
Always carry a spare pad or cloth in your bag, so you are never caught without one.
