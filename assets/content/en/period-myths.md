Many people share ideas about periods that are not true. Here are some common ones.

## "Period blood is dirty."
Menstrual blood is blood from the lining of the womb. It is not dirty. Keep the outside of your body clean with water and mild soap, as you do every day.

## "You cannot exercise or swim on your period."
You can. Light to moderate exercise often eases cramps. With a tampon or menstrual cup, swimming is fine.

## "You cannot get pregnant on your period."
This is **not reliable**. Sperm can live for days, and cycles can be short or irregular, so pregnancy is possible at any time you have sex without protection.

## "Skipping a period means you are fine."
A missed period can mean pregnancy, stress, illness or a hormone change. If you are sexually active and your period is late, take a pregnancy test.

## "Cramps are normal, so just suffer."
Some cramps are normal. Pain that stops your daily life, or that is worse than before, deserves medical advice.

## "Only women who are ill get heavy periods."
Heavy bleeding is common, but it should still be checked if it is very heavy or very long. See the article "When to see a health worker".
