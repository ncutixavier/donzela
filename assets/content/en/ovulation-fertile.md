Ovulation is when an ovary releases an egg. The egg lives for only about **12 to 24 hours**. Sperm can survive in the body for up to about **5 days**. Together, this makes a **fertile window** of about 6 to 7 days that ends on the day of ovulation.

## How Donzela estimates it
- Ovulation is estimated as **14 days before the predicted next period**.
- The fertile window is estimated as **5 days before ovulation to 1 day after**.
- These are averages. Your real ovulation day can move by several days from cycle to cycle, especially in stressful or irregular months.

## Signs you may notice
- Clear, stretchy, slippery discharge, like raw egg white
- Mild pain or pressure low in the belly, on one side
- A small rise in body temperature after ovulation
- A little light spotting

Not every woman notices these signs, and they are not a reliable method on their own.

## Important
Tracking your fertile days does **not** prevent pregnancy by itself. If you do not want to become pregnant, see the contraception articles. If you want to become pregnant, a health worker can tell you more about timing and fertility.
