Simple choices can help you feel better in each phase. These are general tips, not treatment.

## During your period
- **Iron** replaces what you lose. Good sources include beans, leafy green vegetables, eggs, meat and fish.
- Pair iron foods with fruit or vegetables rich in vitamin C, such as oranges or tomatoes, to help absorption.
- Drink water regularly. Warm drinks can be comforting.
- Gentle walking or stretching can ease cramps.

## Before your period (luteal phase)
- Cut back on salt, sugar and very processed food if you feel bloated.
- Eat regular meals to keep energy steady. Hunger can make mood changes worse.
- Calcium from milk, yoghurt or leafy greens may help some women with PMS.

## Mid-cycle
- Energy is often higher. This is a good time for harder workouts if you feel well.

## Everyday habits
- Sleep at regular times. Poor sleep can change your cycle.
- Being very underweight or very overweight can affect your periods.
- Limit alcohol and do not smoke.

If you are unsure about supplements, ask a health worker before you take them.
