Donzela can help you notice changes, but it cannot diagnose. Go to a health centre or hospital if any of these happen.

## Go soon (within a few days)
- No period for **3 months** and you are not pregnant
- Periods that stop, or come fewer than 3 times in a year
- Bleeding between periods, or after sex
- Pain during sex or during urination
- Strong pain low in the belly that is new or getting worse
- Unusual discharge with a bad smell or itching

## Go urgently
- Bleeding that soaks through a pad or cloth **every hour for several hours**
- Bleeding that lasts **more than 7 days**
- Blood clots bigger than a coin
- Fainting, dizziness, a very fast heartbeat or feeling extremely weak
- Severe pain that does not ease, or pain with fever
- A possible pregnancy with pain or bleeding

## If you feel unsafe
If anyone is hurting you or forcing you to have sex, you can get help at the **Isange One Stop Centre (3029)**, or call the ambulance on **912** in an emergency. Both are in the Help tab.

## Bring this to your visit
Open **Raporo y'umuganga** (health worker report) in Donzela. It lists your recent cycles, lengths and symptoms.
