A cycle is called **irregular** when its length changes a lot from one month to the next, for example more than 9 days of difference, or when a cycle is longer than 45 days.

## Common, usually harmless causes
- Stress, exams, grief or a new job
- Changes in weight, very intense exercise or dieting
- Travel, shift work or poor sleep
- Breastfeeding, or recently stopping a hormonal method
- Being a teenager or approaching menopause

## Causes a health worker should check
- Periods that stop for **3 months** when you are not pregnant
- Very long cycles, or periods that come fewer than 3 times a year
- Periods with a lot of hair growth, acne or weight gain
- Symptoms of a thyroid problem, such as tiredness, feeling cold or a fast heartbeat
- Conditions such as polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS), which a clinician can diagnose

## What to do
1. Keep logging in Donzela for at least 3 months.
2. Note any other symptoms in the daily log.
3. Bring your Donzela nurse report to your health centre.

Donzela can show you a pattern, but only a health worker can diagnose a cause.
