Every woman's cycle is a little different. For most adults, a cycle is **21 to 35 days**, counted from the first day of one period to the day before the next. Periods usually last **2 to 7 days**.

## What counts as normal
- Cycle length can change by a few days from month to month. This is normal.
- Stress, travel, illness, sleep changes and big weight changes can make a period come early or late.
- The first cycles after a girl's first period, and the years before menopause, are often irregular.

## How to read your own cycle
- Note the first day of each period. Donzela uses these dates to estimate your next period.
- The more cycles you log, the more accurate the estimates become.
- A cycle that is consistently the same length is "regular". Donzela shows this as higher confidence.

## Important
Donzela's predictions are estimates. They are not a contraceptive and are not a diagnosis. If you want to avoid pregnancy, read the contraception articles.
