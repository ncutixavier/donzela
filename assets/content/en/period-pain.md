Period cramps come from the womb tightening to shed its lining. For many women they are mild. Here is what can help.

## Simple things to try
- **Warmth:** a hot water bottle or warm cloth on your lower belly or back.
- **Movement:** a short walk, gentle stretching, or a yoga pose such as child's pose.
- **Rest:** lie on your side with a pillow between your knees.
- **Fluids:** sip water or herbal tea through the day.
- **Massage:** slow, circular rubbing of the lower belly.

## Pain relief
Pain medicine can help when used as the packet or a pharmacist says. Do not take more than the recommended dose. If you have asthma, stomach ulcers, kidney problems, or you are pregnant, ask a health worker before taking anything.

## When pain is not normal
- It stops you from going to school, work or your daily life
- It is getting worse each cycle
- It starts well before your period or lasts after it ends
- You also have fever, heavy bleeding or pain during sex

These can be signs of conditions such as endometriosis or fibroids. See the article "When to see a health worker".

Track your pain in the daily log. This helps your health worker see the pattern.
