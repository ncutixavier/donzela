Guhitamo ibyo kurya n'ibikorwa bisanzwe bishobora kugufasha kumva umerewe neza muri buri cyiciro. Ibi ni inama rusange, si imiti.

## Mu gihe cy'imihango
- **Fer** isimbuza iyo wataye. Ibiyikungahaye ni ibishyimbo, imboga z'icyatsi, amagi, inyama n'amafi.
- Huza ibiribwa birimo fer n'imbuto cyangwa imboga zirimo vitamine C, nk'indimu cyangwa inyanya, kugira ngo fer yinjire neza.
- Nywa amazi buri gihe. Ibinyobwa bishyushye bishobora kuruhura.
- Kugenda buhoro cyangwa gukora imyitozo yoroheje bishobora koroshya kuribwa.

## Mbere y'imihango (icyiciro cya nyuma)
- Gabanya umunyu, isukari n'ibiryo byinshi byatunganyirijwe mu nganda niba wumva ubyimbye.
- Rya ku masaha ahoraho kugira ngo ufite imbaraga. Inzara ishobora kongera impinduka mu myumvire.
- Calcium iboneka mu mata, yawurute no mu mboga z'icyatsi ishobora gufasha bamwe muri PMS.

## Hagati y'ukwezi
- Imbaraga akenshi ziba nyinshi. Ni igihe cyiza cyo gukora imyitozo ikomeye niba wumva umerewe neza.

## Imyitwarire ya buri munsi
- Ryama no kubyuka ku masaha ahoraho. Gusinzira nabi bishobora guhindura ukwezi.
- Kugira ibiro bike cyane cyangwa byinshi cyane bishobora kugira ingaruka ku mihango.
- Irinde inzoga nyinshi kandi wirinde kunywa itabi.

Mbere yo gufata imiti y'inyongera, baza umukozi w'ubuzima.
