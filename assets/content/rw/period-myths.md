Hari ibintu byinshi bivugwa ku mihango bitari byo. Dore bimwe muri byo.

## "Amaraso y'imihango ni mabi."
Amaraso y'imihango aturuka mu gishishwa cy'imbere mu nda. Ntabwo ari mabi. Komeza hanze y'umubiri wawe hasukuye n'amazi meza n'isabune yoroheje, nk'uko ubigenza buri munsi.

## "Ntushobora gukora siporo cyangwa koga mu mihango."
Urabishobora. Imyitozo yoroheje ikunze koroshya kuribwa. Ukoresheje tampon cyangwa agakombe k'imihango, koga biremewe.

## "Ntushobora gusama mu gihe cy'imihango."
Ibi **ntibyizewe**. Intanga zishobora kubaho iminsi myinshi, kandi ukwezi gushobora kuba gufupi cyangwa guhindagurika, bityo gusama birashoboka igihe cyose ufite imibonano itarinzwe.

## "Imihango itaje bivuze ko uri muzima."
Imihango itaje ishobora kuba ari inda, umuhangayiko, uburwayi cyangwa impinduka mu misemburo. Niba ufite imibonano kandi imihango yawe yatinze, kora ikizamini cy'inda.

## "Kuribwa ni ibisanzwe, wihangane gusa."
Kuribwa bimwe ni ibisanzwe. Ububabare buguhagarika mu buzima bwa buri munsi, cyangwa bukomeje kuba bubi kurusha mbere, bukeneye inama z'umuganga.

## "Imihango myinshi igaragara gusa ku barwayi."
Imihango myinshi irasanzwe, ariko igomba gusuzumwa niba ari myinshi cyangwa imara igihe kirekire. Reba inyandiko "Igihe ugomba kujya kwa muganga".
