Ukwezi bavuga ko **guhindagurika** iyo uburebure bwakwo buhinduka cyane hagati y'ukwezi n'ukundi, nk'iyo itandukaniro ari hejuru y'iminsi 9, cyangwa iyo ukwezi kurenze iminsi 45.

## Impamvu zisanzwe kandi akenshi nta kibazo zitera
- Umuhangayiko, ibizamini cyangwa agahinda
- Impinduka mu buremere, imyitozo ikomeye cyane cyangwa indyo
- Ingendo, akazi k'ibihe bitandukanye cyangwa gusinzira nabi
- Konsa, cyangwa guhagarika vuba uburyo bw'imisemburo
- Kuba uri mu bwangavu cyangwa uri hafi yo gucura

## Impamvu umukozi w'ubuzima akwiye kureba
- Imihango yahagaze **amezi 3** kandi udatwite
- Ukwezi kurerure cyane, cyangwa imihango iza inshuro zitarenze 3 mu mwaka
- Imisatsi myinshi cyane, ibiheri cyangwa kugira ibiro byinshi
- Ibimenyetso by'indwara ya thyroid: umunaniro, gukonja cyane, umutima utera vuba
- Indwara nka PCOS, umuganga ni we uyisuzuma

## Icyo wakora
1. Komeza wandike muri Donzela byibura amezi 3.
2. Andika ibindi bimenyetso mu gitabo cy'ibyo wanditse.
3. Jyana **raporo y'umuganga** ya Donzela ku kigo nderabuzima.

Donzela ishobora kukwereka imiterere, ariko umuganga ni we ushobora kumenya impamvu.
