Kugira isuku mu gihe cy'imihango birinda ubuzima bwawe kandi bigufasha kwiyumva neza. Icyo wakoresha cyose, amategeko ni amwe: komeza hasukuye, uhindure kenshi, kandi ahantu hakomeze humye.

## Udukingirizo tw'isuku (serviyeti)
- Hindura akingirizo buri **masaha 4 kugeza kuri 8**, cyangwa vuba niba yuzuye.
- Banza woze intoki mbere na nyuma yo kuhinduza.
- Hanagura uhereye imbere ujya inyuma.
- Ta udukingirizo twakoreshejwe mu bikoresho byabugenewe, si mu musarani.

## Udukingirizo dukoreshwa inshuro nyinshi
- Banza wozeshe amazi akonje, hanyuma wozeshe isabune.
- Yanika neza ku zuba mbere yo kongera kuyikoresha. Umwenda utose ushobora gutera impumuro n'indwara.
- Komeza ibindi bibiri bisukuye kandi byumye.

## Tampon n'agakombe k'imihango
- Hindura tampon buri **masaha 4 kugeza kuri 8**. Ntuyigumane birenze amasaha 8.
- Agakombe gashobora gukurwamo amazi buri masaha 8 kugeza kuri 12. Kakoze n'amazi meza mbere yo kongera kugashyiramo.
- Niba wumva urwaye cyane, ufite umuriro, kuruka cyangwa gucika intege ukoresheje tampon, uyikure ako kanya kandi ushake ubuvuzi.

## Isuku y'umubiri
- Oza hanze y'ibitsina bya buri munsi n'amazi meza. Ntukoge imbere mu nda cyangwa ngo ukoreshe isabune ikaze.
- Wambare imyenda y'imbere isukuye kandi inyuguza umwuka, uyihindure buri munsi cyangwa igihe itose.

## Jya ufite ibikoresho
Buri gihe ujyane agakingirizo cyangwa umwenda w'inyongera mu gikapu cyawe, kugira ngo utigera usigara nta cyo ufite.
