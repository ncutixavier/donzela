Donzela ishobora kugufasha kubona impinduka, ariko ntishobora gusuzuma indwara. Jya ku kigo nderabuzima cyangwa ku bitaro niba hari kimwe muri ibi bibaye.

## Jya vuba (mu minsi mike)
- Imihango yahagaze **amezi 3** kandi utwite
- Imihango ihagarara, cyangwa iza inshuro zitarenze 3 mu mwaka
- Kuva amaraso hagati y'imihango, cyangwa nyuma yo gukora imibonano
- Kubabara mu gihe cy'imibonano cyangwa igihe uri kunyara
- Kubabara cyane mu nda yo hasi, bishya cyangwa biriyongera
- Ururenda rufite impumuro mbi cyangwa rutera uburyaryate

## Jya byihutirwa
- Amaraso yuzuza agakingirizo k'isuku **buri saha mu masaha menshi**
- Kuva amaraso birengeje **iminsi 7**
- Ibibyimba by'amaraso binini kuruta igiceri
- Kuzirika, kuzunguruka, umutima utera cyane cyangwa kunanirwa cyane
- Kubabara cyane kudashira, cyangwa kubabara hamwe n'umuriro
- Ushobora kuba utwite kandi ufite ububabare cyangwa amaraso

## Niba utumva ufite umutekano
Niba hari umuntu akugirira nabi cyangwa akagusaba imibonano utifuza, ushobora kubona ubufasha kuri **Isange One Stop Centre (3029)**, cyangwa guhamagara imbangukiragutabara kuri **912** mu gihe cy'ihutirwa. Izi nimero ziri mu gice cy'Ubufasha.

## Zana ibi igihe ugiye kwa muganga
Fungura **Raporo y'umuganga** muri Donzela. Yerekana imizunguruko yawe ya vuba, uburebure bwayo n'ibimenyetso.
