Irekurwa ry'intanga ni igihe intanga iva mu ntangangore. Intanga ibaho gusa hagati y'amasaha **12 na 24**. Intanga y'umugabo ishobora kubaho mu mubiri kugeza ku minsi igera kuri **5**. Ibyo bituma habaho **igihe cy'uburumbuke** kingana n'iminsi 6 cyangwa 7, kirangira ku munsi w'irekurwa ry'intanga.

## Uko Donzela ibigereranya
- Irekurwa ry'intanga rigereranywa ko riba **iminsi 14 mbere y'imihango itaha iteganyijwe**.
- Iminsi y'uburumbuke igereranywa ko iba **uhereye ku minsi 5 mbere y'irekurwa ry'intanga kugeza ku munsi 1 nyuma yaryo**.
- Ibi ni impuzandengo. Umunsi nyawo w'irekurwa ry'intanga ushobora guhinduka iminsi myinshi mu kwezi ku kundi, cyane cyane mu mezi arimo umuhangayiko cyangwa ahinduka cyane.

## Ibimenyetso ushobora kubona
- Ururenda rworoshye, rurambuka, rusa n'umweru w'igi ritetse
- Kubabara gato cyangwa kuremererwa mu nda yo hasi, ku ruhande rumwe
- Ubushyuhe bw'umubiri buzamuka gato nyuma y'irekurwa ry'intanga
- Kuva amaraso make

Ntabwo buri mugore abona ibi bimenyetso, kandi ntibihagije ubwabyo nk'uburyo bwo kuboneza urubyaro.

## Ingenzi
Gukurikirana iminsi y'uburumbuke **ntibikumira** gusama wenyine. Niba utifuza gusama, soma inyandiko ku kuboneza urubyaro. Niba wifuza gusama, umukozi w'ubuzima ashobora kukubwira igihe cyiza.
