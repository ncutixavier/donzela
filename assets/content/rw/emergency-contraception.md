Uburyo bwo kwirinda inda mu gihe cyihutirwa bukoreshwa nyuma y'imibonano itarinzwe, cyangwa igihe uburyo bwari bukoreshwa bwanze: agakingirizo kacitse, ibinini byasibwe. **Ntabwo** ari uburyo busanzwe bukoreshwa buri gihe.

## Amahitamo
- **Ikinini cy'ihutirwa (levonorgestrel):** gikora neza cyane mu masaha **72**, kandi gishobora gukora kugeza ku minsi **5** (amasaha 120) nyuma y'imibonano. Gifatwa vuba, gikora neza.
- **IUD y'umuringa:** umukozi w'ubuzima ashobora kuyishyira mu minsi **5** nyuma y'imibonano. Ni yo ikora neza cyane mu bihe by'ihutirwa, kandi ishobora gukomeza gukoreshwa nk'uburyo bumara igihe kirekire.
- **Ikinini cya ulipristal:** gisaba inyandiko y'umuganga, gishobora gukora kugeza ku minsi 5. Baza umukozi w'ubuzima.

## Aho wabibona
- Ibigo nderabuzima n'amafarumasi mu Rwanda bishobora kukugira inama.
- Ntutegereze. Uko ubikora vuba, ni ko bikora neza.

## Nyuma yo kubinywa
- Imihango yawe itaha ishobora kuza kare cyangwa itinze. Yandike muri Donzela.
- Kuruka, kuribwa umutwe cyangwa umunaniro bishobora kubaho. Niba uruka mu masaha 3 nyuma yo kunywa, bwira umukozi w'ubuzima.
- Ntibirinda indwara. Niba uhangayitse, kora ipimwa.

## Utazi niba utwite?
Kora ikizamini cy'inda nyuma y'iminsi hafi 21 uhereye ku mibonano itarinzwe, cyangwa vuba niba imihango yawe yatinze.
